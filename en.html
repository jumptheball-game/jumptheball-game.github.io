FAQ

Is Jump the Ball free?

Yes, the game is free to play. It contains ads and optional in-app purchases (coin packs and “Remove ads”). All worlds and balls can also be unlocked without spending real money.

Which devices is it for?

For iPhone and Android smartphones. Jump the Ball is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

Do I need an account?

No. You start playing right away, your progress is only stored on your device.

How do you play Jump the Ball?

The ball bounces on its own. Swipe left or right to steer it, collect coins for your combo and dodge hazards – in 250 levels or in endless mode.
