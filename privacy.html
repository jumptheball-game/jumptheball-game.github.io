2. In short

Jump the Ball needs no account, runs no servers of its own and uses no analytics or tracking tools. Your game progress stays on your device.

3. Game data on your device

The app stores on your device: coins, unlocks and purchases, level progress, your local leaderboard, settings and the time for refilling lives, plus purchase transaction ids so a purchase is never credited twice. Purpose: providing the game. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; storing it on your device is strictly necessary for the game you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). This data is not transmitted to us and is kept until you delete the app.

4. Download and updates

The app is distributed through the App Store (Apple) and Google Play (Google), which process data under their own responsibility and privacy policies.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We receive no payment data, only confirmation of which product was bought (product and transaction id) to unlock it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app may show ads provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. The advertising id, IP address, device information and ad interactions may be processed to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Personalised ads and access to information on your device only happen with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG), requested on first start; you can withdraw it at any time in the app settings. Google may transfer data to the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (EU adequacy decision of 10 July 2023). Reward videos (e.g. an extra life) are optional. "Remove ads" ends banners and interstitials.

7. Reminders (notifications)

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a reminder on your device for when you have not played for a day. It is triggered locally by the operating system only; no data is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given in the system dialog.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15-21 GDPR), may withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)) and may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77), for example: Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen, Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf
